  "document_text": "# Customer Lifecycle Operations\n\nThis document describes two related but distinct business processes operated by the Customer Platform team: customer onboarding and account provisioning. Provisioning consumes the customer record produced by onboarding.\n\n## Onboarding Purpose\n\nThe customer onboarding workflow registers a new customer: it validates the application, verifies the customer's identity, creates the customer record, and notifies the customer of the outcome.\n\n## Onboarding Trigger\n\nThe workflow starts when a customer application is submitted and an application.received request reaches the Onboarding Service.\n\n## Onboarding Inputs and Outputs\n\nInputs\napplication_id — identifier of the submitted application\nemail — the applicant's email address\n\nOutputs\ncustomer_record_id — identifier of the created customer record\nonboarding_status — registered or rejected\n\n## Onboarding Process\n\nThe Onboarding Service validates the application using application_id\nand returns whether the application is complete.\nIf the application is incomplete, the workflow raises\nApplicationIncomplete and rejects the application; if the application is complete, the workflow continues.\nThe Identity Service verifies the customer identity for email and\nreturns a verification_id.\nThe Customer Service creates the customer record and returns a\ncustomer_record_id.\nThe Notification Service notifies the customer of the registration\noutcome.\n\n## Onboarding Error Handling\n\nApplicationIncomplete: the application is missing required fields →\nreject the application and end the workflow.\nIdentityCheckFailed: identity verification fails → reject the\napplication and end the workflow.\n\n## Onboarding Retries\n\nVerify the customer identity: retry up to 3 times with exponential\nbackoff starting at 2 seconds.\nNotify the customer: retry up to 5 times; failure is non-fatal.\n\n## Provisioning Purpose\n\nThe account provisioning workflow prepares a registered customer's account for use: it reserves an account number, configures the account, and activates it. If activation fails after configuration, the configuration is rolled back.\n\n## Provisioning Trigger\n\nThe workflow starts when a customer record is registered and an account.provision request reaches the Provisioning Service. It requires the customer_record_id produced by customer onboarding.\n\n## Provisioning Inputs and Outputs\n\nInputs\ncustomer_record_id — identifier of the registered customer record\nplan_code — the subscription plan to provision\n\nOutputs\naccount_id — identifier of the activated account\nprovisioning_status — active or failed\n\n## Provisioning Process\n\nThe Provisioning Service reserves an account number for\ncustomer_record_id and returns an account_id.\nThe Provisioning Service configures the account for plan_code.\nIf the configuration is invalid, the workflow raises\nConfigurationInvalid; if the configuration is valid, the workflow continues.\nThe Provisioning Service activates the account and returns the final\nprovisioning_status.\n\n## Provisioning Error Handling\n\nConfigurationInvalid: the plan configuration cannot be applied → roll\nback the provisioning (release the account number) and end the workflow.\nActivationFailed: activation does not complete → roll back the\nprovisioning (deconfigure the account, release the account number).\n\n## Provisioning Retries\n\nConfigure the account: retry up to 3 times with exponential backoff\nstarting at 1 second.\n\n## Provisioning Compensation\n\nRelease the account number compensates Reserves an account number —\nreturn the reserved number if provisioning is rolled back.\nDeconfigure the account compensates Configures the account — undo the\nplan configuration if provisioning is rolled back after configuration.",
