## Purpose
The Payment Reconciliation workflow verifies that every captured payment matches the order total and the payment gateway settlement record, and files a discrepancy report when they disagree.

- end states: reconciled, discrepancy_reported

## Retries
- Fetch settlement record: up to 3 times with exponential backoff starting at 5 seconds
- Fetch order total: up to 3 times with exponential backoff starting at 5 seconds

## Activities
- [a1] Fetch settlement record
- [a2] Fetch order total
- [a3] Compare amounts
- [a4] Mark reconciled
- [a5] File discrepancy report and notify

## Decisions
- [d1] Do amounts match? — after: a3; yes: a4; no: a5

## Open Questions
- [x] (R6-bounded-waits) What deadline bounds each wait (e.g. 24 hours)?
  Answer: Settlement and order-total fetches are bounded by their retry policies; no long waits — the whole reconciliation must finish within 1 hour
- [x] (R7-exceptions) Which failures should the workflow handle, and what are they named?
  Answer: SettlementUnavailable (settlement record fetch exhausts retries); OrderTotalUnavailable (order total fetch exhausts retries)
- [x] What is the deadline for waiting on carrier pickup confirmation in the Order Fulfilment workflow?
  Answer: Not applicable to this workflow — pickup confirmation is now synchronous at dispatch in order-fulfilment (24-hour window recorded there)
- [x] Which failures should the Payment Reconciliation workflow handle and what are they named?
  Answer: SettlementUnavailable and OrderTotalUnavailable, as above
